# Marketing video storyline: the CV Screening Assistant journey

The story runs in seven scenes, grouped into five fragments. `video/src/timeline.json` holds the timing, the fragments and the data each scene shows. The seconds and narration below match it, and `test/marketing_video_timeline_test.py` checks that.

From the canvas scene to the mitigations scene, each scene ends on the button that starts the next one, and the next scene's first words bridge from it. The narration never describes a click: the ticks, the save button and its toast show the choice.

| Scene | Seconds | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| hook | 6 | Amazon's hiring AI allegedly ranked women lower. Scrapping it took three years. | The incident title, cited to the AI Incident Database, incident 37, over a timeline: 2014 development starts, 2015 bias against women found, 2017 project abandoned |
| problem | 9.5 | Today, you can't afford to spend years building AI that causes harm. The challenge is to govern AI responsibly and create real value. | The hook's three-year bar returns with marks for time, effort and harm as the statement is voiced; then two circles, "Govern responsibly" and "Create real value", overlap into one target under the challenge in the brand colour |
| canvas | 9.5 | Meet AI Center X. Map your initiative on the AI Canvas: its business problem, its data and the people it affects. | "Define an initiative", AI Canvas excerpts with the business problem, data and people affected lit as they are named, then the initiative page with "Simulate risks" |
