# Marketing video storyline: make AI deliver value

**Message: make AI deliver value. Govern your AI risks, accelerate your projects.**

The story runs in six scenes, grouped into five fragments. `video/src/timeline.json` holds the timing, the fragments and the data each scene shows. The seconds and narration below match it, and `test/marketing_video_timeline_test.py` checks that.

The opening states the pitch deck's problem: AI ambition rarely becomes business value. Each figure keeps the deck's wording and source, so no claim is wider than its study. The product then shows how a CV Screening Assistant's risks are governed in under an hour. The video says nothing about the roadmap.

From the canvas scene to the mitigations scene, each scene ends on the button that starts the next one, and the next scene's first words bridge from it. The narration never describes a click: the ticks, the save button and its toast show the choice.
