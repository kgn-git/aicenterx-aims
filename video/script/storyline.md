# Marketing video storyline: the CV Screening Assistant journey

The story runs in seven scenes, grouped into five fragments. `video/src/timeline.json` holds the timing, the fragments and the data each scene shows. The seconds and narration below match it, and `test/marketing_video_timeline_test.py` checks that.

From the canvas scene to the mitigations scene, each scene ends on the button that starts the next one, and the next scene's first words bridge from it. The narration never describes a click: the ticks, the save button and its toast show the choice.
