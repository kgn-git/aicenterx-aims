# Marketing video storyline: make AI deliver value

**Message: make AI deliver value. Govern your AI risks, accelerate your projects.**

The video is a 30-second pitch in four beats: the world (0–5 s), the break (5–15 s), the fix (15–25 s) and the proof (25–30 s). `video/src/timeline.json` holds the timing, the fragments and the data each scene shows. The seconds and narration below match it, and `test/marketing_video_timeline_test.py` checks that.

The people are real, filmed in real work settings: stock footage from Pexels, listed in `video/src/footage.json`. Each figure keeps the qualifiers of its source, so no claim is wider than its study. The product appears only in the fix, as a fast montage of the CV Screening Assistant's risks and mitigations.
