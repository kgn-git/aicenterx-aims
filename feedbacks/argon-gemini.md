### 2. What Changed & Why (Marketing Breakdown)

* **Cut the "Click-by-Click" Filler:** Deleted repetitive phrases like *"You select the relevant ones and save them"* (0:36), *"Again, you select and save"* (0:50), and *"You save the ones you need"* (1:03). The viewer can see the checkboxes being clicked on screen; narrating it makes the voiceover sound like an automated training manual.


* **Trimmed Agent Loading Screens:** The original video shows the "AI Agent" checklist animation three separate times (at 0:30, 0:42, and 0:55). Showing it once at 0:30 proves how the agent thinks; skipping it on the next two steps saves **10 seconds** and makes the software feel instantaneous.


* **Elevated the Hook & Transitions:** Instead of stating facts dryly, the script connects the dots: Problem (Amazon's 3-year failure) $\rightarrow$ Tension (Governance vs. Speed) $\rightarrow$ Resolution (Simulating risks before coding $\rightarrow$ Turning policy into engineering).

### 3. How to Fix the "Robotic" Voiceover

The current voiceover sounds robotic because it has **flat pitch contours**, **uniform pauses between clauses**, and **over-enunciates acronyms** (like "M-I-T" and "E-U-A-I Act"). If you are using an AI voice generator (such as ElevenLabs, Murf, or OpenAI), apply these adjustments:

1. **Use Punctuation for Micro-Pauses & Inflection:**
* Replace periods with **em-dashes (`—`)** when you want the voice to hold tension rather than dropping its pitch completely (e.g., *"building an AI hiring tool—only to scrap it"*).
* Add **ellipses (`...`)** before big reveals to create a slight human breath pause (e.g., *"Three years of trial and error... reduced to less than an hour."*)


2. **Adjust Voice Model Settings (e.g., in ElevenLabs):**
* **Lower "Stability" to 35%–45%:** High stability (70%+) forces the AI into a monotone, newsreader cadence. Lowering it introduces natural human vocal inflection and warmth.
* **Set "Style Exaggeration" to 15%–25%:** Gives the voice a subtle "storyteller" lift without sounding overly dramatic.
* **Choose a "Conversational" or "Narrative" Voice Profile:** Avoid voices tagged as "News" or "Explanation." Look for warm, mid-range baritone or alto voices tagged for *B2B Commercial* or *Documentary*.


3. **Phonetic Spelling for Smooth Acronyms:**
* If the TTS engine stumbles on acronyms, write them phonetically in the prompt: write `"E-U A-I Act"` or `"ISO forty-two thousand and one"` (if read aloud) so the cadence doesn't stutter.
