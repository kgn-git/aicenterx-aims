A Periodic Table of AI Benchmarks

Columns group benchmarks by the primary construct they measure. Rows place them on the innovation sequence, from invention to diffusion. Press play to watch the table fill year by year, and notice how little of it reaches adoption and diffusion, where entrepreneurs and adopting firms need evidence most.

Click a tile to open the benchmark, its description and how it is classified. In the window, the arrow keys step through the tiles currently shown. Click a dimension to highlight benchmarks that carry it as a primary or secondary code. Coloured dots on a tile mark its secondary dimensions.
