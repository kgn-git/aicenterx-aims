The LinkedIn post draws on three sources, numbered as on the image.

1. **PwC, *29th Global CEO Survey*, January 2026** (4,454 CEOs in 95 countries). Used for two figures:
   - **56%** of CEOs report no significant financial benefit from AI so far (the opening line).
   - **3×**: CEOs with strong AI foundations are three times as likely to report meaningful financial returns. PwC defines those foundations as responsible AI and risk processes, a clear AI roadmap, an enabling technology environment and an enabling culture.
   - Links: [press release](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/news-room/press-releases/2026/pwc-2026-global-ceo-survey.html) and [full report (PDF)](https://workplaceinsight.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Report-PwC-2026-Global-CEO-Survey-Strictly-Embargoed-5.45PM-GMT-Monday-19-January-2026.pdf).

2. **McKinsey, *The State of AI*, August 2026** (1,719 respondents in 97 countries). Used for **75%**: about 75% of AI high performers have fundamentally redesigned workflows, against about 25% of other organisations.
   - Link: [The state of AI](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights/the-state-of-ai).

3. **BCG, *How Agents Are Accelerating the Next Wave of AI Value Creation*, December 2025**. Used for **70%**: BCG's 10-20-70 rule, under which 70% of the work in an AI transformation is people and processes, 20% technology and 10% algorithms.
   - Link: [BCG article](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/agents-accelerate-next-wave-of-ai-value-creation).
